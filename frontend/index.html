Know what the results mean

Automatic masks are estimates, especially for multiple subjects, edge-to-edge scenes, or off-center objects. Transparent PNGs use their existing alpha channel. Inspect the cutout before relying on its palette.

Swatches are cluster averages, not exact original pixel colors. Their shares describe the selected mask at analysis resolution (up to 1600 px). Hex search sums swatch shares within a CIELAB ΔE76 tolerance; color families use HSV rules. CLIP similarity is a ranking score, not a probability.

Model downloads happen only during AI setup. No API key is required; uploaded images and inference remain on your computer.

Meet your smarter media library.

1. Bring your images. Upload product shots, illustrations, or campaign images. ChromaDAM stores a resized analysis copy on your computer.

2. Inspect the subject. Open any card to compare the original and cutout. Refine the mask with a foreground click when needed.

3. Find your match. Combine a description with a color family or custom hex and minimum share. Try “shoe + at least 40% red”. In CLIP mode, results are ranked by semantic similarity.

4. Take the colors with you. Copy swatches, download the cutout, export JSON or Tailwind tokens, and test contrast in the color lab.

AI setup. Run install-ai.cmd from the project folder, then restart the app. The pipeline page reports model availability and runtime errors. Without models, the app clearly labels GrabCut and keyword fallback modes.

Project scope. This is a single-user local application. Keep it bound to localhost. Shared hosting needs authentication, upload quotas, and a Python inference service.
